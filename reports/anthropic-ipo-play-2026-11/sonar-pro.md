## Demand and “priced-in” risk

The IPO is reportedly oversubscribed, but the available result does not provide a verifiable subscription multiple or independently confirmed allocation data.[5] The $70–$78 range values Anthropic at up to approximately **$1.4 trillion**, while reporting says that is below some earlier expectations of $2 trillion.[2] That combination creates two opposing signals:

- **Positive:** large offering, reported demand, and participation by Google, Nvidia, and AMD.[4]
- **Negative:** the lower-than-rumored valuation may represent an already cautious market-clearing price rather than upside, and the scale of the offering may absorb capital from existing technology stocks.[2]

Thus, the IPO may already be partly priced into GOOGL, especially because the strategic investment has been reported before the public debut. A successful ANT debut could also cause a **sell-the-news** reaction: investors who bought GOOGL on the partnership or IPO reports may sell after pricing or after ANT opens.
