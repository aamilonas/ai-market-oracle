# Kimi K3.1 Pro

## Bottom line

**My pick is Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN), but only as a small, speculative event trade.** It is the best available proxy because it combines an existing Anthropic investment with substantial AWS business tied to Anthropic. It is **not** a clean Anthropic tracker: if you require high conviction, the better decision is not to force a proxy trade.

### What I verified

- Anthropic’s amended filing offers **825 million shares at $70–$78**. That implies proceeds of **$57.75 billion–$64.35 billion**, with a fully diluted valuation of up to **$1.405 trillion** at the top. People familiar with the process cited November 18 pricing and November 19 trading, but those were plans rather than an issuer-confirmed timetable. [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html) [fathom.news](https://www.fathom.news/anthropic-etfs-graniteshares-ninepoint/)
- Bloomberg subsequently reported demand of approximately **six times the shares offered**, with allocations expected to favor existing backers. That is encouraging, but orders are not the same as final allocations or guaranteed aftermarket buying. [stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/news/anthropic-ipo-six-times-oversubscribed/)
- **Do not treat $78 as the final IPO price yet.** As of Sunday, the detailed contemporaneous reports still describe pricing as scheduled for Wednesday.
- CNBC identifies Amazon as an early backer whose stake **could** be worth hundreds of billions of dollars, without giving a sufficiently precise current ownership percentage to calculate an exact Amazon NAV uplift. Anthropic also has a commitment to spend **at least $110 billion with Amazon over a decade**. A successful IPO improves Anthropic’s funding capacity and validates the value of that relationship. [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/anthropic-ipo-is-make-or-break-for-the-technology-industry.html)

At the top of the range, Anthropic’s headline valuation would be approximately **46% above** the $965 billion valuation announced in May. However, that comparison and the IPO terms have been public since November 10, so some valuation read-through is already priced in.

### Expected move

My base case is approximately **+2% to +4%** for AMZN from entry through the exit, with a favorable tail of roughly **+5%** if pricing is strong, ANT opens sharply higher, and the broader AI tape is simultaneously risk-on. A flat or negative result despite a successful ANT debut is entirely plausible because the Amazon linkage is indirect.

## Why sell on the debut rather than hold through November 23?

The Amazon thesis depends on **valuation validation and improved confidence that Anthropic can fund its AWS spending**. Final pricing and ANT’s opening print resolve much of that uncertainty. Afterward, Amazon is again driven principally by its own earnings, cloud growth and the broader market.

ANT itself has powerful technical support that **does not transfer mechanically to AMZN**:

- Only about **6% of Anthropic’s outstanding shares** are being offered.
- Reuters reports expected inclusion in at least six major indexes within **5–15 days**, with estimated passive demand of roughly **$19 billion**, plus retail allocation of about 20%.
- In the SpaceX comparison, shares rose sharply through day three and remained above their IPO price during the initial 15-day period before drifting lower as lock-up concerns approached.

Those are reasons ANT itself might remain strong after its debut—not reasons to keep holding Amazon. Reuters also notes potential first-day Anthropic employee selling and no lock-up on the separate private-placement shares, adding uncertainty. [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/analysisfrom-spacex-to-anthropic-markets-have-a-new-playbook-for-megashare-debuts-5020374)

I could not verify a reliable, repeatable pattern of AMZN or GOOGL moving sympathetically around SpaceX’s debut, so I am not assuming such a pattern.

## What would make me abandon it?

Exit immediately, or do not enter if the news arrives first, when any of these occurs:

1. **The IPO is postponed, materially downsized, or priced below $70.**
2. **AMZN reaches the 3% stop**, regardless of the Anthropic headline.
3. Nvidia’s Tuesday results or guidance produce a broad AI selloff that overwhelms the Anthropic read-through. Nvidia is an especially large unrelated risk this week after its recent sharp decline. [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/anthropic-ipo-is-make-or-break-for-the-technology-industry.html)
4. **ANT opens below its final IPO price and cannot reclaim it within approximately 15 minutes.**
5. ANT prices and opens strongly but AMZN remains flat-to-down. That means the expected transmission mechanism is not working; do not invent a longer-term justification for keeping the trade.

## Biggest risks

- **Buy-the-rumor, sell-the-news:** the price range, potential $1.405 trillion valuation, strategic investments and heavy oversubscription are already public. The final pricing may produce little additional information.
- **Weak proxy transmission:** index funds forced to buy ANT will not be forced to buy AMZN.
- **No immediate cash realization:** an IPO valuation does not automatically mean Amazon sells its stake or receives cash. I could not verify Amazon’s precise ownership, lock-up terms or accounting treatment from the available reports.
- **Long-dated commercial benefits:** the AWS commitment is spread over a decade. Anthropic’s commitments exceed $900 billion overall, so its enormous spending requirements are both the opportunity and the funding risk.
- **Sector risk dominates:** Nvidia’s results and broader AI volatility could easily move Amazon more than the IPO does.
- **Misreading the strategic investments:** AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC collectively buying **$26 billion of Anthropic stock** is an investment by those companies—not $26 billion of immediate revenue for them. That alone is not a reason to buy their shares. [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html)

**Runner-up: Alphabet (GOOGL).** It has Anthropic exposure and Anthropic intends to spend $125–$150 billion on Google AI chips, but deliveries are not expected to begin until 2028, making that benefit less immediate for a one-week trade. Amazon’s combined equity-stake/AWS linkage is cleaner.
