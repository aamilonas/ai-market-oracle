#### The Rationale: Highest Asymmetric Exposure
When trading an IPO proxy, raw nominal stake size matters far less than the **ratio of the stake’s value relative to the proxy company’s market capitalization**:
* **Amazon (AMZN)** and **Alphabet (GOOGL)** hold the largest nominal stakes in Anthropic (~$142B–$270B and ~$132B–$139B, respectively). However, AMZN is a ~$2.88 trillion mega-cap grappling with massive 2026 capital expenditures (~$220B) and convertible note caps, while Alphabet carries a ~$3.5T+ market capitalization. Anthropic represents only ~3% to 7% of their total equity value, blunting any direct price reaction.
* **Zoom Video Communications (ZM)**, by contrast, holds an early strategic stake from its 2023 Series C investment ($51M) and early 2026 follow-on ($46M), totaling a cost basis of just ~$97 million. Zoom carried this stake on its July 31, 2026 Form 10-Q at **$3.135 billion** (marked against the $965B Series H valuation). At Anthropic's $74 midpoint / $78 top of the range (or a modest debut pop towards $1.2T–$1.4T), Zoom’s holding is worth **$3.9 billion to $4.8+ billion**.
* Against Zoom’s current market capitalization of **$29.12 billion** and net cash pile of ~$7.2 billion, its Anthropic position accounts for **~14% to 17% of Zoom’s total market value** and roughly **20% to 25% of its enterprise value (~$21.9B)**.
* Stripping out cash and the liquid fair value of the Anthropic asset, the market is pricing Zoom's core operational platform (which generates ~$1.9B in annual free cash flow) at an implied single-digit EV/FCF multiple. The ANT pricing and debut will force institutional analysts and automated models to mark Zoom's balance sheet to market, driving an immediate rerating.
