Based on the SpaceX blueprint, the peak of the early‑trading pop occurred on the third trading day (+50% by day 3) [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/analysisfrom-spacex-to-anthropic-markets-have-a-new-playbook-for-megashare-debuts-5020374). - **Expected move:** 5–8% from Monday’s open to Monday’s close (based on typical retail‑brokerage rallies around blockbuster IPOs).  
- **Stop‑loss:** I’d place a stop 3% below my entry price. If HOOD reverses sharply before the IPO prices (e.g., on Tuesday), I’m out.  
- **Abandon triggers:**

- **Market interference:** Nvidia’s Nov 18 earnings (the same day as pricing) could overshadow the IPO’s impact. A bad result would likely drag all tech‑adjacent names, including HOOD.
