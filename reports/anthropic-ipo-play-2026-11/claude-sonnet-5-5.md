I'll start by checking the reported IPO news and the related ownership details.**Bottom line:** I'd buy Amazon (AMZN). The edge is small and I'm not confident in it. The macro backdrop is hostile, and the Anthropic stake is too small relative to Amazon to move the stock much on its own.

## Verification of the reported news
The timetable checks out.
- CNBC reports the company plans to price on Nov. 18 and trade on Nov. 19.
- The offering is 825 million shares at $70–$78.
- The European prospectus gives the same indicative dates, which remain subject to change.
- Pricing is not final until the final prospectus is filed.
- Demand looks strong. Bloomberg reported orders at about six times the shares available. The FT reported bankers expect to price above the range.
- Neither report is confirmed by the company. EDGAR showed no filing after Nov. 10, so the range is still $70–$78.

## 1. What to buy: AMZN (Amazon), about $266.66
This is the most direct large-cap, liquid way to own the IPO mark-up.
- **Largest stake:** Amazon will hold about 1.92 billion non-voting Class N shares after the offering, worth about $149.8B at $78.
- **More to come:** Another 1.56 billion shares, worth about $122B, will be issued later because of a 12% ownership cap.
- **Relative exposure:** Amazon's stake equals about 9% of its market value, against 4% for Alphabet.
- **It also sells compute to Anthropic:** Anthropic committed at least $110B to AWS through April 2036.
- **Not yet run up:** AMZN closed Nov 13 at $266.66. That is about 7.7% below its 52-week high.
- **Relatively resilient:** On Nov 12, Nvidia fell 7.6% while Amazon rose 2.3%. On Friday Amazon fell only 0.3%. That is a thin sample, though.

**Why not the others**
- **Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC (placement buyers):** Their placement checks are tiny relative to their size. At the midpoint, Google and Nvidia buy about $10B each, AMD $2.5B, and Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft about $1B each. The bigger issue is that Nvidia reports Nov 17, the day before pricing. Options imply a roughly 9% swing. That is a coin flip that has nothing to do with Anthropic.
- **Alphabet (GOOGL):** Its stake is similar in dollars, but it is only about 4% of its value. It also has its own overhang. Alphabet booked an $8.7B loss tied to the SpaceX drop in Q3, and shares fell more than 12% on Oct. 29 after an earnings miss.
- **Closed-end funds and ETFs (DXYZ, AGIX):** They hold Anthropic, but premiums on closed-end funds holding IPO candidates tend to collapse after the underlying lists. Their NAV premiums are not something I could verify today.
- **Space-stock precedent:** The SpaceX debut is a warning about proxies that don't own the stock. Rocket Lab, Redwire and Intuitive Machines each dropped at least 10% as SpaceX began trading.

## 2. When to buy
**Monday, Nov 16, about 10:00 AM ET, with a limit order.**
- I'd skip the opening auction. The Empire State survey is at 8:30 and Fed President Williams speaks at 9:05.

## 3. When to sell
**Primary exit: Thursday, Nov 19, about 3:45 PM ET, on the first trading day.** Sell before the close rather than holding into Friday.
- **Why then:** Amazon's stake gets marked to a real ANT price that day. Holding past it adds "sell the news" and fund-rebalancing risk with no new catalyst.
- **Index funds:** Nasdaq's fast-entry rule would add ANT to the Nasdaq-100 around Dec 21. That means index funds would sell other holdings later. This falls outside our window but is a reason not to linger.
- **Early exit:** If AMZN hits my target before then (say Wednesday), take the profit.
- **Why not Wednesday:** The SpaceX precedent argues for selling before listing day. Space stocks gave back most of Thursday's rally as traders sold the news. I'd still hold into Thursday, but I'm less sure of it than of the entry. Those were non-owners, and the evidence on owners is mixed. One source says EchoStar surged 11% on debut. Another says it dropped 9%.
- **Hard limit:** Out by the Nov 23 close either way.

## 4. Expected move, stop-loss, and abandon conditions
- **Expected move:** About +1% to +2%, with a plausible range of roughly −4% to +4%.
- **How I got that:** A 20% ANT pop on Amazon's roughly $142B stake at $74 is about $28B. That is only about 1% of Amazon's roughly $2.88T market value. The stock's own daily noise is larger.
- **Target:** about $274 (+2.7%).
- **Stop-loss:** about $259 (−2.9%). Friday's intraday range alone was about 2.3%, so a tight stop can be hit by noise.
- **Abandon the trade if:**
  - The IPO is postponed, or it prices at or below $70.
  - Nvidia's Tuesday report crushes AI sentiment and AMZN gaps down more than 3% on Wednesday.
  - The 10-year yield spikes further. It was at 5.15%.
  - AMZN rises more than 4% before Wednesday on no news. Take that profit.

## 5. Biggest risks
- **Macro:** Nvidia just posted its sixth straight weekly decline after the CEO's resignation. A Fed hike in December is fully priced. Democratic midterm gains raised regulatory worries for AI. That backdrop can swamp any Anthropic effect.
- **Funding drain:** After SpaceX, VandaTrack said selling of AI winners may have funded retail purchases. Anthropic is raising far more than SpaceX did, so mega-cap AI names could be sold to fund it. This is my inference.
- **Small stake effect:** Anthropic is under 10% of Amazon's value, and a bad AWS quarter would matter more than a great Anthropic one.
- **Already priced in?** Partly.
  - The above-range pricing is already reported, so it is not a surprise.
  - Amazon has not run up, so the rumor-buying is not obviously done.
  - One data set cited by TradingKey shows average 60-trading-day returns of −6% for buying on a first day. I couldn't see its methodology.
  - Since 1980, IPOs priced above range gained 50% on day one on average. That helps ANT more than Amazon, which only gets a fraction of the pop.
- **Weak debut:** One investor warned the worst outcome for the AI bubble would be an underperforming Anthropic IPO. A flat or negative debut would likely hit Amazon too.
- **Stale data:** Prices are from the Nov 13 close. I could not verify anything after Friday.

I put the chance this trade is profitable at my Thursday sell at only about 52%. If you want a trade with better odds, there isn't one here. The honest alternative is to skip it.
